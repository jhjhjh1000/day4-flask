Advanced English Proficiency: Upcoming Mock Examination Schedule and Registration

person
Dr. Alistair Vance
calendar_today October 24, 2024
visibility 1,248 Views

Dear Students,

As we approach the mid-semester evaluation period, the Department of English Language and Literature is pleased to announce the schedule for the mandatory Mock IELTS and TOEFL Examinations. These sessions are designed to provide a realistic assessment environment to prepare you for official certification paths.

Key Dates

  • check_circle IELTS Listening & Reading: Nov 12, 09:00 AM
  • check_circle IELTS Writing: Nov 13, 02:00 PM
  • check_circle TOEFL Integrated Session: Nov 15, 10:00 AM

Registration for these sessions will open via the Scholar Board portal starting tomorrow at 08:00 AM. Please ensure your student profile is up to date and that you have completed the prerequisite "Grammar & Synthesis" module (Level B2+).

Failure to participate in the mock exams may impact your final grading for the Academic Writing component. If you have any scheduling conflicts, please contact the administrative office no later than Friday.

Regards,
Academic Excellence Committee
